The camera path, end to end, on the hardware the app ships to:
iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 26.6, Release configuration, 2026-08-30.

Produced by the app itself. The fixtures are pushed into the container, so
nothing about this test is compiled into the binary:

    xcrun devicectl device copy to --device <udid> \
      --domain-type appDataContainer --domain-identifier Lulu.solvr-ai \
      --source docs/fixtures/camera --destination Documents/fixtures
    xcrun devicectl device process launch --console --device <udid> \
      Lulu.solvr-ai -- -SolvrCameraSelfTest YES

Attach the console. Launched without one the app is suspended between
generations, and a run that had taken 4.8s and 3.5s for steps and lesson took
110s and 408s instead -- same weights, same phone, same answers. Worth knowing
before quoting an on-device latency at anyone.

Two lines are the point of the whole exercise. "asked 4 surfaces about one
question" is requirement one, on the device. The KaTeX line is requirement two,
measured by loading each surface's real page into a real WKWebView with the
vendored KaTeX and `throwOnError: true` -- not by checking the strings somewhere
else and assuming the page agreed.

The third thing it says is the uncomfortable one: Vision read the integral sign
as a bullet and dropped the exponent, so the model was asked about
`• 3x + 2x + 4 dx` and answered that, correctly, in 4.8 seconds. On this path the
model is rarely the problem.
